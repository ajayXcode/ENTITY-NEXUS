Champion index

Seventeen champions.
Eight archetypes.

Each champion is a fixed combat identity that the cabinet will build for you, and every card below is rendered from the game's own parsePrompt() — so nothing on this page is a promise the fight cannot keep. RAPHAEL and MALGRAVE also ship their own battle sprites, which is what the portrait on their cards is. Pick one in the cabinet and its strategy is written into your box, or cast a line out of the dialogue vault and the same parser builds the same kind of fighter from the line instead.
