The roster

Seventeen champions.
Eight ways to win.

Every champion is a fixed combat identity — species, style, weapons, role and traits — and its stats are read off its strategy by the game’s own parser, so a card can never claim something the cabinet would not build. Two of them, RAPHAEL and MALGRAVE, fight in their own sprites. Field one and its strategy is written for you, or cast a line out of the dialogue vault and the same parser builds a fighter out of the line instead. All eight archetypes the parser can produce are on the roster.

The machine

Five doors.
One simulation.

A deterministic 60 FPS fight with no input once the bell rings: the whole round is a pure function of both lines and a seed, so it can be replayed and audited rather than trusted. The floor runs a pari‑mutuel pool on ArenaBattle, and a settled winner mints on Monad with its line, seed and result on the token.
