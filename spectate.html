HOW THE POOL PAYS

  1. Both fighters stake. Equal stakes into ArenaBattle. The winner takes 1.5× their stake, the loser gets half of theirs back — losing costs you half, not everything.
  2. The crowd backs a side. Every bet joins that fighter’s pool. Betting stays open until the match settles, and the two players may not bet on themselves.
  3. Settlement splits the losing pool. 5% goes to the protocol. The other 95% is shared across the winning pool in proportion to each bet.
  4. You claim it yourself. Payouts are pull‑based: claimSpectatorPayout() returns your stake plus your share. Back the winner and your principal always comes back.

If nobody backed the winner, the losing side is refunded in full instead — no one is paid out of an empty pool. If nobody backed the loser, there is no profit to share and winning bets simply return their stake.
